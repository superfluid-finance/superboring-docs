# Referrals

## Introduction

SuperBoring offers a referral program that allows users to earn a \$BORING commission on the users referred to the plaform.
This is a great way to earn passive \$BORING by simply referring new users to the platform.

## How does it work?

The referral program is simple: you refer a new user to the platform, and you earn a commission on the fees generated by the referred user.
In order to refer a user, you need to provide them with your referral link. When the user opens a position and starts earning \$BORING using your referral link, they become your referred user
and you earn **5% of their total \$BORING earned**.

### Example
- Alice refers Bob to the platform using her referral link.
- Bob opens a position and starts earning \$BORING.
- Alice earns **5% of the total \$BORING earned** by Bob.
