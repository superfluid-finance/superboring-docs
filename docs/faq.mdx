# FAQ

## Who are **Liquidity Movers**?
- Users who facilitate trades for incoming streams. They compete with each other closing trades through our Liquidity Solution using GDA’s instant distribution, and receive a fee in exchange.

## What is **Liquidity Solution**?
- A configurable service run by Liquidity Movers, which automatically settles trades for regular users.

## What is the **GDA**?

- GDA (General Distribution Agreement) is a primitive of the Superfluid Protocol and a Super Power of the Super Tokens.
To learn more about GDA, please refer to the [Superfluid Protocol Documentation](https://v2.docs.superfluid.finance/docs/protocol/distributions/overview).
