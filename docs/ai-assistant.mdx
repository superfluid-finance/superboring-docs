# AI Assistant (experimental)

:::warning Experimental feature
The AI Assistant is an early experimental feature. Behavior, pricing, and limits may change as we iterate.
:::

The **AI Assistant** is an in-app agent that helps you research, build, and operate DCA strategies on SuperBoring. It is paid for natively in \$BORING, spent directly out of your [Sleep Pod](/docs/boring#sleep-pods).

## What you can do with it

- **Chat** — ask questions about markets, TOREXes, your positions, and SuperBoring itself.
- **Strategy compilation** — describe a DCA strategy in natural language; the Assistant produces an executable plan.
- **Strategy execution** — opt into running a compiled strategy. The Assistant evaluates it periodically and acts on your behalf.

## How you pay for it

Under the hood, the Assistant is metered through **DIEM**. From the user's perspective, you just see a daily AI balance, denominated in \$BORING.

### Enabling the Assistant

To enable the AI Assistant on your account, you spend \$BORING from your Sleep Pod. Spending is a one-way **burn-to-treasury** transfer:
the Sleep Pod sends \$BORING to the SuperBoring treasury, which the team periodically converts into DIEM credits to fund the underlying AI service.

You do not need to manage DIEM yourself. The mapping from "\$BORING spent" → "AI capacity" is configured in the backend and may be tuned over time.

### Your Daily AI Balance

Once enabled, you'll see three numbers in the UI for the AI Assistant:

- **Daily AI Balance** — your total daily capacity (e.g. 100).
- **Consumed Daily AI Balance** — how much of today's capacity you've already used.
- **Pending AI Balance** — reserved-but-not-yet-consumed capacity (e.g. by a compiled strategy that hasn't fully run).

The UI presents this as a compact triple, for example:

```
0/100 (0)        ← fresh day: 0 consumed of 100, 0 pending
30/100 (0)       ← after chatting: 30 consumed, 100 capacity, 0 pending
30/80 (20)       ← compiled a strategy: 20 capacity is now pending
5/100 (0)        ← next day: capacity refilled, 5 consumed by a periodic strategy execution
```

:::tip Daily refills, not a wallet
**The Daily AI Balance refills every day** — it is a usage quota, not a withdrawable balance. You cannot move it out, swap it, or convert it back to \$BORING.
This mirrors how DIEM works under the hood.
:::

### What costs what

| Action | Cost |
|--------|------|
| Chat message | Consumes Daily AI Balance per message (variable, based on size). |
| Strategy compilation | **Flat fee**, charged once when you compile. The fee is reserved as *Pending AI Balance* on top of being consumed. |
| Strategy execution (periodic) | Consumes Daily AI Balance each time the strategy ticks. |

Exact \$BORING-per-unit and the flat compile fee are **backend-configurable settings** and will be tuned during the experimental phase.

## Smart-contract surface

The on-chain footprint of the AI Assistant is intentionally minimal:

- **Burning \$BORING:** the Sleep Pod sends \$BORING to the treasury address. No custom logic — this is a plain transfer.
- **View function:** any user's current AI balance is readable from the contracts so the UI and integrators can display it.

All metering, refilling, and strategy state lives off-chain in the SuperBoring backend.

## Operational notes

- The team periodically converts treasury-held \$BORING into DIEM credits, which back the live AI capacity.
- During the experimental phase, expect Daily AI Balance limits, per-action costs, and the \$BORING-per-AI-credit ratio to be adjusted based on real usage.
- Feedback on the Assistant is highly encouraged — it directly shapes how pricing and limits evolve.
