# Why integrate SuperBoring?

## Introduction
In order to offer a DCA service to your users, you can integrate SuperBoring into your platform.
Integrating SuperBoring allows you to achieve the following:
- Offer a DCA service to your users
- Earn a commission on the fees generated by the users that go through your interface.
- Earn a referral bonus in \$BORING depending on the users you refer to SuperBoring.

In the next section we will explain the fees and rewards you can earn by integrating SuperBoring.

## What is a Distributor and how much do they earn?

A Distributor is an operator of a SuperBoring interface who integrates SuperBoring on their platform.
A Distributor earns through the **Referral Fee**: a 5% commission on the \$BORING rewards earned by the users they refer to SuperBoring.

:::info Frontend Fee has been removed
Earlier versions of SuperBoring split the 0.5% TOREX fee between $BORING stakers and the interface operator (the *Frontend Fee*).
As of SuperBoring contracts v2.0.0, the distribution-fee module has been removed: the full 0.5% TOREX fee now goes to $BORING stakers.
Distributor revenue is now exclusively the referral commission described above.
:::

To learn more about this, please refer to the [$BORING page](/docs/boring) and the [Referrals page](/docs/referrals).
