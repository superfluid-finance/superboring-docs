# Introduction

Welcome to SuperBoring, our innovative Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA) application designed to leverage the power of Superfluid streams. SuperBoring is developed by the Superfluid team on top of the Superfluid Protocol, a cutting-edge protocol pushing the boundaries of money transactions on the blockchain.

## What is SuperBoring?

SuperBoring is an application that simplifies and enhances the DCA strategy for cryptocurrency investments. DCA is a method where investors divide up the total amount to be invested across periodic purchases of a target asset to reduce the impact of volatility on the overall purchase. SuperBoring takes this concept to the next level by integrating it with Superfluid's unique streaming technology.

## The SuperBoring Advantage

- **Efficiency and Accessibility**: SuperBoring makes DCA more efficient and accessible. By using Superfluid's streaming technology, it allows for continuous and automated investment flows, reducing the need for manual intervention and making investment more seamless and user-friendly.
- **Reduced Volatility Impact**: The core idea of DCA is to mitigate the risks associated with volatile market conditions. SuperBoring enhances this approach by allowing for more frequent and smaller investments, thereby further reducing the potential impact of sudden market changes.
- **Innovation in DeFi**: By leveraging Superfluid's groundbreaking streaming technology, SuperBoring stands at the forefront of innovation in decentralized finance, offering a unique approach to cryptocurrency investments.

## How it works

SuperBoring has a very unique mechanism allowing it to offer a new and improved DCA experience.
The application is built on top of Superfluid's streaming technology, which allows for continuous and automated money flows.
This means that users can open a stream of stablecoins and have it automatically split into smaller chunks which will be sold to arbitrageurs
at a small discount following Uniswap's Time Weighted Average Price (TWAP).

1. **User Opens a Stream**: Any user can open a stream of stablecoins to start investing in their preferred cryptocurrency.
2. **Arbitrage**: The stream will be automatically split into smaller "chunks" which will be sold to arbitrageurs (called **Liquidity Movers**) at a small discount following Uniswap's Time Weighted Average Price (TWAP).
3. **Set it and forget it**: No further actions required from the user. Thanks to Superfluid's Money Streaming, the user will receive the most accurate average price of the asset over the period of the stream.
